# Shipping Policy

Pawl delivers to every province and city in Vietnam from our warehouse in Ho Chi Minh City.

## 1. Delivery options

| Option | Fee | Delivery time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard delivery | 30,000₫ | 2–4 working days |
| Express delivery (same day, inner city) | 45,000₫ | Same day for orders placed before 15:00 in the inner districts of Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi |

The fee is shown at checkout before you pay. Delivery times are counted from the moment your order is confirmed (and, for bank transfer orders, paid). Remote areas and public holidays may add 1–3 days.

## 2. Processing

- Orders are packed every day from 8:00 to 18:00.
- Orders placed after 18:00 are packed the next morning.
- You receive an email when your order is confirmed. Your order status is always visible in **My account → Orders**.

## 3. Receiving your order

- The courier calls the phone number given at checkout before delivery. Please keep your phone reachable.
- The courier tries to deliver up to 3 times. If delivery fails 3 times, the order is returned to us and cancelled; prepaid amounts are refunded minus the shipping fee.
- Please check the package in front of the courier. If it is torn, crushed or leaking, take a photo and contact us within 48 hours (see the [Returns & Refunds Policy](/policies/returns)).

## 4. Address

Make sure your address includes the province or city, the ward or commune and the house number and street. We are not responsible for delays caused by an incomplete address, but we will contact you to fix it.
