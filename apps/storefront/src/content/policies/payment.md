# Payment Policy

Pawl offers two ways to pay. All prices are in Vietnamese dong (VND) and include VAT.

## 1. Cash on delivery (COD)

Pay the courier in cash when your order arrives. Please prepare the exact amount shown in your order confirmation. COD is available for every delivery address.

## 2. Bank transfer by QR code

1. Choose **Bank transfer (QR)** at checkout and review your order.
2. Scan the QR code with any Vietnamese banking app (or transfer manually to the account shown).
3. Transfer **the exact amount** and keep **the exact transfer note** shown on the payment page (for example `PAWL7K3M9XQ2`). The note is how we match the payment to your order.
4. Press **I have completed the transfer**. Your order is created as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Each QR code is valid for **15 minutes**. If it expires, request a new one; do not reuse an old transfer note. If you change your cart, a new code is issued for the new amount.

If you transferred money but your order was not created, contact us with a screenshot of the transfer. We will create the order or refund you in full.

## 3. Security

- Pawl never asks for your card number, online banking password or OTP.
- The payment page is only reached from checkout on pawl.vn. Ignore messages that ask you to pay into any other account.

## 4. Invoices

Every order comes with an email confirmation. If you need a VAT invoice for a company, email support@pawl.vn with your order number and company details within the month of purchase.

See the [Returns & Refunds Policy](/policies/returns).
