# Privacy Policy

Pawl Vietnam Co., Ltd. (demo) ("Pawl", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it and what rights you have, in line with the Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP.

## 1. Data we collect

| When | Data |
| --- | --- |
| You create an account | Name, email, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash) |
| You place an order | Contact email and phone, delivery addresses, the products you bought, the payment method you chose |
| You browse the website | Your shopping cart and the technical data needed to keep you signed in (a session cookie) |
| You contact us | Your name, email and the content of your message |

We **do not** collect or store card numbers or online banking passwords. We do not collect sensitive personal data.

## 2. Why we use your data

- To process, deliver and support your orders (performing our contract with you).
- To manage your account, saved addresses and order history.
- To send service emails: order confirmation, delivery updates, password reset.
- To prevent fraud and keep the website secure.
- To meet our legal obligations, for example accounting and tax records.

We only send marketing messages if you agree to receive them, and you can stop them at any time.

## 3. Your consent

When you create an account or place an order you are asked to agree to this policy. The time of your consent is recorded. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us; this does not affect processing that already took place, but we may no longer be able to serve some requests (for example, keep an account open).

## 4. Who we share data with

- **Carriers**, who receive your name, phone and delivery address to deliver your order.
- **Banks and payment partners**, who process your payment.
- **Hosting providers**, who store our database and images on secure servers.

We never sell your personal data. Partners may only use it for the service they provide to Pawl.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Account data: as long as your account is open, and up to 12 months after you ask us to close it.
- Order and invoice data: 10 years, as required by accounting law.
- Contact messages: 24 months.

## 6. Security

Connections to our website and database are encrypted. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it to serve you.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent and to complain. You can update your name, phone and addresses yourself in **My account**. For anything else, email **support@pawl.vn**; we answer within 72 hours.

## 8. Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and the browser's local storage to remember your shopping cart. We do not use advertising cookies.
