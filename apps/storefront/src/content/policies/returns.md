# Returns & Refunds

We want you and your pet to be happy with every order. If something is wrong, we will make it right.

## 1. When you can return an item

| Situation | Time limit | Who pays return shipping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong item, damaged or leaking package, expired product | Report within **48 hours** of delivery | Pawl |
| Changed your mind (item unopened, seal intact) | Within **7 days** of delivery | You |
| Accessory with a manufacturing defect | Within **30 days** of delivery | Pawl |

## 2. Items we cannot take back

For hygiene and pet safety reasons we cannot accept the return of food, treats, supplements or litter that have been **opened**, unless the product is faulty. Items bought on clearance are marked "final sale" on the product page.

## 3. How to return

1. Contact us by hotline **1900 0000** or email **support@pawl.vn** with your order number.
2. For damaged or wrong items, send a photo or a short unboxing video.
3. We confirm within 24 hours and arrange a pickup or tell you where to send the item.

Please check your package when the courier hands it over. For cash on delivery orders you may refuse a package that is clearly damaged.

## 4. Refunds

- After we receive and check the item, we refund the price of the item (and the shipping fee when the return is our fault).
- Orders paid by bank transfer are refunded to the same bank account within **5–7 working days**.
- Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer to the account you give us.
- Instead of a refund, you can choose an exchange for the same or another product.

## 5. Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge until it has been handed to the carrier. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number.
